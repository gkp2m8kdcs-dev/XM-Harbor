Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how XM Harbor (the "App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use it. The short version: XM Harbor does not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter into the App stays on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

XM Harbor is an on-device personal finance journal. We do not collect, store on a server, or transmit to any third party:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address)
  • Financial account credentials or login information
  • Brokerage, exchange, bank, or wallet connections
  • Transaction records, holdings, budgets, or goals
  • Receipt photos or attachments
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or advertising data
  • Location data, contacts, calendars, or biometric data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you create in XM Harbor — including transactions, holdings, lots, cost-basis figures, accounts, categories, budgets, goals, settings, and receipt photos — is stored locally on your iPhone in a private JSON-based store. This data is:

  • Saved only to your device's local file system and protected by iOS sandboxing.
  • Never sent off-device by the App under any circumstance.
  • Accessible only to you through the App's interface.
  • Removable at any time via the in-app reset feature, or by uninstalling the App.

Standard iOS data protections (such as device passcode / Face ID / Touch ID and Full Disk Encryption) apply to this data as they do to any file on your device.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

XM Harbor requests only the operating-system permissions necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Camera: used solely to attach a receipt photo to a transaction you are creating. Photos are saved locally and never leave your device.
  • Photo Library: used solely to attach an existing photo as a receipt. Photos are saved locally and never leave your device.

You can decline either permission and continue using all other features of the App. If you decline, the receipt-attach feature will be unavailable, but no data is collected in either case.

4. No Account, No Cloud, No Sync

XM Harbor does not require you to create an account. There is no sign-up, no login, no email verification, and no password. The App does not provide cloud sync, backup, social, sharing, or export-to-server features. All data lives on the device on which it was entered.

5. No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with, embed, or call any third-party analytics, advertising, marketing, attribution, social, or crash-reporting service. There are no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App does not contain advertising of any kind.

6. Children

XM Harbor is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children, because we do not collect any information from anyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. Your Rights

Since all data lives only on your device, you have full control at all times. You can:

  • View, edit, or delete any record inside the App.
  • Use the in-app one-tap reset to erase all stored data.
  • Uninstall the App to permanently delete the App and all its local data from your device.

Because we hold no data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retrieve, modify, or delete on your behalf.

10. Disclaimer

XM Harbor is a personal journaling and tracking tool. It does not connect to any brokerage, exchange, wallet, or financial institution, does not place trades, and does not custody any assets. All figures shown in the App are computed solely from entries you make. XM Harbor is not a financial advisor and does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice.
