Your money, in one place.

XM Harbor is a personal finance journal and portfolio tracker for investors who want a clear, on-device view of where their money actually is. No brokerage connection. No cloud. Just your numbers.

◐

Net-Worth Overview

Open with a net-worth hero, a cash-flow sparkline, and your Liquid versus Invested split at a glance.

≡

Activity Journal

Transactions grouped by day with quick filters, search, and one-tap receipt attachment from camera or photo library.

▦

Multi-Lot Portfolio

Track every holding, lot, cost basis, and average cost across stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, real estate, and commodities.

◍

Asset Mix

Interactive donut chart you can tap to drill into. Built from your entries — never from a brokerage feed.

⌐

Custom Reports

Six-month cash-flow, monthly income versus expense, daily spending trend, category breakdown, and account distribution — drawn as SwiftUI charts.

◎

Goals & Budgets

Set a target, see a progress bar. Stay minimal — and stay on track.

Private by design.

XM Harbor is on-device only. No account. No cloud. No analytics. No ads. No tracking of any kind. All transactions, holdings, budgets, goals, accounts, and receipt photos live in a private store on this iPhone.
