XM Harbor — Help & Contact

Thanks for using XM Harbor. This page covers the most common questions and how to reach us if you need help.

About the App

XM Harbor is an on-device personal finance journal and portfolio tracker. All transactions, holdings, budgets, goals, accounts, and receipt photos are stored locally on your iPhone. The App does not connect to a brokerage, exchange, wallet, or cloud service, and does not require an account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your iPhone in a private, sandboxed JSON store. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If you uninstall the App or reset your device, that data is removed.

How do I back up my data?

Because XM Harbor does not sync to a cloud, your iPhone's standard iCloud or iTunes/Finder device backup is the recommended way to retain your data across device transfers or resets. Backups are managed entirely by Apple's iOS system, not by XM Harbor.

How do I delete all my data?

Open the App, go to the Settings tab, and use the one-tap reset option. Alternatively, uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data.

Does XM Harbor connect to my brokerage?

No. XM Harbor does not connect to any brokerage, exchange, bank, or wallet. All figures shown are calculated from the entries you make inside the App. XM Harbor does not place trades and does not custody any assets.

Why was I asked for Camera or Photos permission?

These permissions are used solely to attach a receipt photo to a transaction you create. You can decline either permission and continue using every other feature. Photos are saved locally and never leave your device.

Does the App contain ads or track me?

No. There are no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What devices are supported?

XM Harbor requires iOS 16 or later and is designed for iPhone.

Troubleshooting

  • Receipt photo won't attach: make sure you've granted Camera or Photos permission in iOS Settings → XM Harbor.
  • Numbers look off: remember that all figures are computed from your entries. Check the underlying transactions, lots, and cost-basis values.
  • App behaves unexpectedly after an iOS update: please update XM Harbor to the latest version from the App Store, and restart your device.
